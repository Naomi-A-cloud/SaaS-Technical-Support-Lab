# Ticket Lifecycle

## 1. Ticket Creation

A customer reports an issue through the support channel.

The support engineer records:

- Customer information
- Product area
- Issue description
- Business impact
- Initial severity

## 2. Initial Investigation

The support engineer:

- Reproduces the issue where possible
- Collects relevant information
- Reviews application behaviour
- Checks logs when available
- Identifies potential causes

## 3. Troubleshooting

The engineer follows a structured troubleshooting process.

Actions and findings are documented throughout the investigation.

## 4. Resolution

Once the issue is resolved:

- The solution is documented
- The customer is informed
- The ticket is updated
- Any required follow-up is recorded

## 5. Escalation

Issues are escalated when they require access, expertise or authority beyond the support team's scope.

Examples include:

- Backend service failures
- Security-related events
- Infrastructure problems
- Widespread service outages
- Persistent application defects

## 6. Closure

A ticket can be closed when:

- The issue is resolved
- The customer has been informed
- Troubleshooting evidence is documented
- Required escalation has been completed
