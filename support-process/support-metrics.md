# Support Metrics

## Key Performance Indicators

### First Response Time

Measures how quickly Support acknowledges a customer issue.

### Time to Resolution

Measures the time required to resolve a support ticket.

### First Contact Resolution

Measures how many issues are resolved without escalation.

### Escalation Rate

Measures the percentage of tickets requiring another technical team.

### Ticket Reopen Rate

Measures how often previously resolved tickets are reopened.

### SLA Compliance

Measures whether tickets are handled within their defined service targets.

## Why Metrics Matter

Support metrics help identify:

- Recurring technical problems
- Training gaps
- Documentation gaps
- Product reliability issues
- Escalation trends
- Customer-impacting problems

## Technical Support Principle

Metrics should be used to improve the support process rather than simply measure individual performance.
