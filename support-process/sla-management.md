# SLA Management

## Purpose

Service Level Agreements define expected support response and resolution targets.

## Priority Levels

### P1 - Critical

Major outage or severe business impact.

Target: Immediate response and continuous investigation.

### P2 - High

Significant functionality affected.

Target: Rapid response and active investigation.

### P3 - Medium

Limited customer impact with a workaround available.

Target: Standard support response.

### P4 - Low

General questions, minor issues or requests.

Target: Standard queue handling.

## SLA Monitoring

Support engineers should monitor:

- Time to first response
- Time to investigation
- Time to resolution
- Escalation time
- Customer communication intervals

## SLA Risk

A ticket may become an SLA risk when:

- Investigation is delayed
- Engineering escalation is pending
- Customer impact increases
- Required information is missing
- The ticket remains unresolved near its target time

## Support Practice

SLA management should focus on both technical resolution and clear customer communication.
