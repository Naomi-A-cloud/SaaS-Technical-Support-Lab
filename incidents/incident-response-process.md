# Incident Response Process

## 1. Detect

Identify unusual behaviour through:

- Customer tickets
- Monitoring alerts
- Logs
- Application errors

## 2. Assess

Determine:

- Scope
- Severity
- Customer impact
- Affected service

## 3. Declare

Create an incident when the issue has significant or widespread impact.

## 4. Escalate

Notify the appropriate technical teams.

## 5. Investigate

Collect:

- Logs
- Error messages
- Timestamps
- Affected endpoints
- Customer impact information

## 6. Mitigate

Apply temporary measures where possible to reduce customer impact.

## 7. Resolve

Restore normal service.

## 8. Verify

Confirm that the service is functioning correctly.

## 9. Document

Record:

- Timeline
- Root cause
- Impact
- Resolution
- Lessons learned

## 10. Close

Close the incident after service recovery and documentation are complete.
