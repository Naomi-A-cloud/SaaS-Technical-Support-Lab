# INC-001: Authentication Service Outage

## Incident Information

Incident ID: INC-001  
Severity: P1 - Critical  
Service: Authentication  
Status: Resolved

## Summary

Multiple CloudDesk customers reported that they were unable to authenticate.

## Customer Impact

Users were unable to access the CloudDesk platform.

## Detection

Support tickets increased rapidly and multiple customers reported similar authentication failures.

## Investigation

Support reviewed authentication logs.

The investigation identified repeated authentication-service failures affecting multiple users.

## Initial Assessment

The issue was determined to be service-wide rather than isolated to individual customer accounts.

## Escalation

The incident was escalated to the engineering and incident-response teams.

## Resolution

Engineering restored authentication-service functionality.

Support confirmed successful authentication from affected accounts.

## Root Cause

Simulated backend authentication-service failure.

## Lessons Learned

- Multiple similar tickets can indicate a wider incident.
- Support should correlate tickets by symptom and timeframe.
- Authentication incidents require rapid escalation.
- Customer communication is important during widespread outages.

## Final Status

Resolved.
